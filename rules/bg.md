| Limited liability company (ООД), several managers | Each manager represents the company alone, unless the partnership agreement provides otherwise. Other limits on the manager's powers have no effect against third parties. | Art. 141(2) Commercial Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Joint-stock company (АД), board of directors or management board | Members represent the company collectively, unless the articles provide otherwise. One or more members can be authorised to represent it. Limits on their powers have no effect against third parties and are not entered in the register. | Art. 235(1), (2), (4) Commercial Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Procuration (прокура) | May be exercised separately or jointly. Signatures are notarised and the procuration is entered in the register. | Art. 21(1) Commercial Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Procurator and real estate | A procurator cannot transfer or encumber real estate without a special authorisation. | Art. 22(2) Commercial Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Procuration, effect against third parties | Takes effect against third parties after it is entered in the register. | Art. 24 Commercial Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Commercial attorney | The power of attorney is in writing with a notarised signature. A restriction can be invoked against a third party only if the third party knew it or should have known it. | Art. 26(1)-(2) Commercial Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Register entry, reliance | Third parties acting in good faith can rely on an entry. An entry that is missing is treated as non-existent. | Art. 10(1)-(2) Commercial Register Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
| Register entry, proof | Courts, state and local authorities, persons with public functions and organisations providing public services, including banks, may not require proof of facts already entered in the register. | Art. 23(6) Commercial Register Act | 26 Oct 2026 |
